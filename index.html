About

Analytics that starts on the ground.

I spent three years in IT support and systems analysis at Sky Bridge Logistics before moving into performance analysis — so I know where operational data comes from, and where it breaks.

Today I monitor KPIs across the Sky Bridge Group: delivery times, fleet efficiency and site volume in logistics; fuel sales across retail sites; room revenue, ADR and related hotel metrics in hospitality. I turn them into dashboards, weekly analyses and recommendations for management.

I care about the plumbing as much as the picture: clean extracts, documented measures, and reports people trust enough to act on.
